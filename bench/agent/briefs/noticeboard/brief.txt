Build a community noticeboard for a small town. Anyone can browse notices, search them, and filter them by category: events, for sale, lost and found, and services. Each notice has its own page, with a form for replying to it. Replies are kept for moderators to read and pass on. Moderators sign in to add, edit and remove notices and to read replies.

There's no email anywhere: replies are stored, not emailed, and sign-in isn't by email. This is a public demo, so seed it with example notices and replies, and show the demo moderator's sign-in details on the sign-in page.
