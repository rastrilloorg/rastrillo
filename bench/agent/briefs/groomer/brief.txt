Build a booking app for a small dog-grooming salon. Customers pick a service and a time slot, then book with their name, phone number and their dog's name. The owner signs in to manage services, opening hours and bookings.

There's no email anywhere: no confirmation emails and no email sign-in. This is a public demo, so seed it with example services and bookings, and show the demo owner's sign-in details on the sign-in page.
